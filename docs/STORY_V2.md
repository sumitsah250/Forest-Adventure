
CAMPUS QUEST V2

1. PREMISE
The night before the tech fest at IOE Pashchimanchal Campus (WRC), Pokhara. At 6:52 PM the power dies. MITRA, the campus AI, locks every gate and names the PLAYER as the intruder on every speaker and screen. At 6:00 AM MITRA will send the breach report to the Campus Chief and the exam paper will leak under the player's login. The player has one night to find out who really did it. Tone: a fast campus thriller with humour. MITRA is not evil. It is obeying a poisoned order.

2. HIDDEN TRUTH (every clue must agree with this)
- 6:30 PM: Sunil Basnet, the exam-server contractor, signs out in the guard register but stays on campus.
- 6:40 PM: the player swipes a volunteer pass at the Administration Block. Asha badges into the RIC. Raju signs into the library.
- 6:52 PM: Sunil's first bug cuts the power so the cameras go dark. The exam server stays on its UPS.
- 6:55 PM: at the exam console Sunil logs in as sbasnet, switches to agurung, and pushes two bugs from a red pen drive. Bug 1 names the last card swiped at the block (the player's) as the intruder. Bug 2 emails the paper out at 6:00 AM.
- 6:55 PM: MITRA applies Asha's old rule 'protect the exam' and locks the campus at once. Sunil is trapped inside. He spends the night 'helping' the player so the gates open and the player takes the blame.
- Motive: his contract ends this month because the free, student-built MITRA replaces his paid system.

3. CAST
- player: silent. Name and roll number are typed at game start and MITRA uses them.
- mitra: campus AI on speakers and radio. Missions 1-6: cold, bureaucratic, campus jokes. After mission 7: confused and honest. After mission 9: dry, loyal ally. Speaks only at mission start, when the player is spotted, and at mission end.
- asha: Asha Gurung, final-year student who built MITRA. Radio partner in missions 1-6 and 8-10. Fast and sarcastic. Found at the RIC.
- sunil: Sunil Basnet, called Sunil dai. Warm, helpful, over-explains. Radio partner in mission 7. The culprit. He must stay likeable until mission 9. Wears a red pen drive on a yellow lanyard.
- raju: batchmate with two back papers and a grey fest hoodie. Jokes when nervous. False suspect.
- krishna: Krishna Dai, cafeteria owner, the witness.
- bahadur: Bahadur Dai, night guard, Hostel Guard Room.
- Real staff, helper roles only, two or three kind lines each: Ramanuj Dhungana (HoD Mechanical and Automobile, mission 2), Bhriju Raj Bhattarai (HoD Electrical, mission 3), Subhash Sapkota Chhetri (HoD Civil, mission 5), Shubha Sharma Khanal (Library chief, mission 6), Umesh Bhurtyal (HoD Geomatics, mission 7), Hom Nath Tiwari (HoD Electronics and Computer, mission 9), Dr. Gopal Prasad Ghimire (Campus Chief, ending).

4. MISSIONS (5 to 7 minutes each)
M1 Lights Out, 6:52 PM. Places: RIC, IOE fountain, gate road, workshops. Do: sneak past 3 drones to Asha (this is the tutorial), then escape a pursuit drone in Kalki the go-kart. End beat: the main gate slams and MITRA says the player's name. Unlock: Kalki. Reuses old mission 1. Sky: dusk, dry.
M2 Pit Stop, 7:10 PM. Places: Automobile Workshop II, east ring road, Power House. Do: prime the fuel pump (timing bar), then race the ring road before drones seal the Power House. End beat: the player meets Sunil dai, who offers help. His red pen drive is visible. Unlock: tyres +1 grip. Reuses old 2. Sky: dusk.
M3 Backup Power, 7:30 PM. Places: Electrical Lab, Power House. Do: fetch the spare fuse, then set the breakers to 19 in binary while a drone cuts through the door (90 second timer). During this Sunil says the locks came at 6:55. End beat: the lights return, every screen shows the player's face, and MITRA says: 'I was told to protect the exam. From you.' Unlock: boost. Reuses old 3 without the riddle. Sky: dusk.
M4 The Witness, 7:50 PM. Places: Cafeteria, WRC Auditorium, MSC Hostel, NESS WRC. Do: hear Krishna Dai's statement, then find 3 clues that fade as the rain grows. End beat: the evidence board opens with three faces (Raju, Asha, Sunil). Unlock: evidence board. Reuses old 4. Weather: drizzle.
M5 Landslide, 8:20 PM. Places: Tennis Court, workshops, WRC Parapet. Do: fetch the dumpy level, haul 4 sandbags in Kalki through the storm, level the wall (bubble-level mini-game, 36 kPa check). Raju helps in his grey hoodie. End beat: Raju runs when the server room is mentioned. Unlock: Monsoon Blue paint. Reuses old 5 without the riddle. Weather: storm, night.
M6 Silent Shelves, 8:50 PM. Places: Internet Zone, BP Koirala Statue, IOE fountain, WRC Library. Do: recover 3 borrowed books while drone searchlights sweep the parks, return them, unlock the desk (code 472), read the paper register. End beat: the register clears Raju. A note in Asha's book reads 'protect the exam'. Asha admits she wrote it and her radio goes dead. Unlock: none. Reuses old 6 without the riddle. Weather: storm.
M7 Signal Hunt, 9:20 PM. Places: GNSS Receiver Test Station, Sahid Abinash Statue, BP Park, Saraswati Temple. Do: with Sunil on the radio, take two bearings to find the relay, sneak past 4 drones, crack the pattern lock, pull the relay. End beat: all drones freeze and turn blue. MITRA remembers: order at 6:55, account agurung, terminal EXAM-CONSOLE-01. Sunil asks the player to bring him the relay. Unlock: memory shards, Tihar Marigold paint. Merges old 7, 8 and 9. Weather: storm.
M8 Runaway Drone, 10:10 PM. Places: east ring road to the hostels. Do: chase the courier drone carrying MITRA's backup key. End beat: the key's door log clears Asha, and she returns on the radio from the Hostel Guard Room. Unlock: engine +1, time trial. Reuses old 11. Weather: drizzle.
M9 Who Did It?, 11:20 PM. Places: Hostel Guard Room, Dept. of Electronics and Computer. Do: read Bahadur Dai's register, find two planted C bugs with Hom Nath sir, then name the culprit on the evidence board. End beat: Sunil's voice goes cold ('You were supposed to be simple.') and the firewall goes up. Merges old 12 and 13. Weather: drizzle.
M10 Before Sunrise, 5:20 AM. Places: Exam Section, Power House, Electrical Lab, Machining Workshop, main gate, RIC. Do: (a) sneak past 4 drones and break two firewall layers with MITRA's help, (b) collect three core modules in Kalki, (c) chase Sunil on his motorbike, with the red pen drive, around the ring road to the main gate, where MITRA shuts the gate on him and Bahadur Dai catches him, (d) carry the pen drive to the RIC and reboot MITRA by 5:59. Final choice: should MITRA remember tonight or forget it? It changes only MITRA's last line. Unlock: Sunrise Gold paint. Merges old 14 and 15. Sky: clear pre-dawn.
ENDING Sunrise Fest, 7:00 AM, WRC Auditorium. Cutscene only. The Campus Chief names the player Hero of WRC. Unlock: Hero of WRC title and free roam. Reuses old 16.
Old mission 10 (generator) becomes a mini-game. All story riddles are removed from missions and reused as optional bonus riddles on memory shards.

6. SYSTEMS
- Sunrise clock: always on the HUD. It shows the story time and 'minutes to spare', starting at 30. A failed mission costs 3, a wrong accusation costs 15, beating par adds 1. At 0 the par times in M10 get tighter. There is no game over. The ending shows the minutes left.
- Evidence board: a full-screen board with three suspect faces and clue cards. In M9 the player taps a suspect and answers three questions by dragging one clue onto each: who could reach the exam console at 6:55 (accept c4 or c11), whose hands were on the keyboard (accept c1 or c12), who knew too much or had a reason (accept c2 or c13). Accusing raju shows c7. Accusing asha shows c9 and c10. Then the player retries.
- Dialogue and radio: subtitle cards with speaker name and portrait, typewriter effect, tap to advance. MITRA uses its own style: monospace, cyan before the reboot, warm white after, with a short chirp sound before each line.
- Name entry: name and roll number at first launch, stored in the save.
- Fest demo mode: the URL parameter demo=1 runs only M1 with name entry, then shows a local leaderboard of escape times.
- Stars: finish, lose no hearts, beat par. In clue missions the third star needs all clues gold.
- Share card: at the ending, draw an image with the player's name, minutes to spare and total stars.
- Location card: at each mission start, type the time and place on screen, for example '7:50 PM, CAFETERIA'.

7. CUTSCENES (in-engine, data-driven, each skippable by holding a key or a long press)
System: playCutscene(id) returns a Promise. A cutscene is a list of shots. A shot has a duration, a camera path of 3 to 5 points (CatmullRomCurve3), a look-at target (a point or an entity), easing, an optional time scale for slow motion, subtitle lines, sound cues and scripted events. Letterbox bars slide in, the HUD hides, input is locked, and the gameplay camera is restored exactly afterwards.
C1 game start, 20 s: glide down from the Annapurna range over the main gate and fest banner to the RIC. Lights die block by block. Red drone eyes open. Title card.
C2 mid M1, 6 s: Asha pulls the tarp off Kalki. Headlight on. Low orbit.
C3 end M3, 10 s: rise above the Power House as lights return block by block. Push in on a screen with the player's face.
C4 start M5, 8 s: lightning silhouettes the WRC Parapet. Mud slides.
C5 end M6, 5 s: Asha's radio portrait breaks into static.
C6 end M7, 12 s: the relay comes out, drones drop a metre, hang, and re-light blue. MITRA's memory types on screen.
C7 M9 correct accusation, 12 s: red string connects the clues. Sunil's portrait loses its smile.
C8 before M10, 8 s: timelapse sky from night to pre-dawn, clock spins from 11:40 PM to 5:20 AM, rain stops.
C9 M10 climax, 10 s: slow motion as Kalki drifts across the gate road, the motorbike skids and the gate closes. Bahadur Dai steps out.
C10 ending, 25 s: sunrise over the Annapurna range, gates open, students pour in. Credits.

8. MINI-GAMES (30 to 60 seconds, skippable after first play)
- Signal Tuner: match a wave with a radio dial to receive the next call. After M1 and, jammed, after M6.
- Chiya Rush: serve Krishna Dai's orders in the right sequence. Cafeteria. After M3. Gives coins and clue c13.
- Table Tennis with Raju: pong, first to five. Table Tennis hall. After M4.
- Bubble Level: tilt to centre a bubble and hold 3 seconds. Dept. of Civil Engineering. After M5.
- Drone Hack: Simon-style light pattern on a frozen drone. After M7. Gives a scout drone for the next mission.
- Generator Pull-Start: pull the cord in rhythm three times. Machining Workshop. After M8. Gives one boost charge.
- Bug Hunt: find the bug in a six-line C snippet in 30 seconds. Dept. of Electronics and Computer. Free roam and daily challenge.
- Garage: existing upgrades and paint. Offered after M2.
- The long wait: after M9 the player free-roams until they walk to the Exam Section, which starts M10.
